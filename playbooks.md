# Avenue Playbooks — four ways to a first stranger-dollar

Pick ONE avenue plus a runner-up (the day-10 pivot target). Your agent can iterate sub-ideas
inside the avenue, but the avenue itself is fixed after launch — decided at kickoff.

## The hard filter first: no threshold-gated platforms

Platforms where monetization unlocks only after an audience threshold **cannot pay inside 4 weeks**:
YouTube Partner Program needs ~1k subscribers + thousands of watch-hours; X payouts need Premium plus
follower/impression minimums. "Trendy Shorts about the World Cup" is a real business — on someone
else's 6-month clock. Pick formats that can take money on **day one**: your own store, your own bot,
your own service, a closed market. (Raised and settled at the kickoff — don't relearn it.)

Also settled: **no borrowed audiences.** The bot doesn't get your channel or your follower base —
insider revenue doesn't count anyway.

---

## 1) Productized service (wrap something you already do well)

Package a capability you already built or know (Mike's insurance-landing flow, a niche analysis,
a report generator) as a fixed-price service the agent sells and fulfills digitally.

- **Time to first dollar:** days — the moment a Payment Link meets a real buyer.
- **KYC:** Stripe account (owner, one-time) or Telegram Stars if delivered via bot.
- **ToS landmines:** none if you sell on your OWN site/bot. Marketplaces (Upwork, Fiverr) ban bots — don't.
- **Toolset:** site generator, Stripe Payment Links, AgentMail for inbound.
- **First probe:** one landing page + one $5-20 fixed-scope offer; agent drives traffic via ToS-clean surfaces (its own SEO pages, its own directory listings — no cold DMs without your gate).
- **Kill criterion:** no checkout click in 7 days of a live page → reprice or pivot the offer.

## 2) Print-on-demand storefront (designs on the news cycle)

Agent designs merch "на злобу дня", a POD provider (e.g. Printful) prints/ships, storefront sells.

- **Time to first dollar:** days-weeks; POD margin is thin (~$5-15/item), so it's a volume-of-attempts game.
- **KYC:** storefront + POD accounts (owner), payment processor.
- **ToS landmines:** **trademarks** — World Cup was the live example: FIFA marks, team crests, player likenesses are all protected; "fan-adjacent" wordplay only. POD providers auto-reject some IP; don't rely on it.
- **Toolset:** image gen (external cost → ledger!), Printful API, storefront.
- **First probe:** 5 designs on ONE news moment, one product type, $0 ad spend — organic surfaces only.
- **Kill criterion:** 100+ unique visitors with zero sales → the designs, not the traffic, are the problem.

## 3) Telegram bot / mini-app on Stars (the closed loop)

Agent builds and operates a paid bot or mini-app: news-of-the-day game, niche utility, content bot.
Discussed variants at kickoff: engaging game around daily news; ad-slot resale via TG ad exchanges;
generated-content bots (mind item 4 of the constitution — no impersonation, and generated-persona
content must stay inside platform ToS and the group's ethics gates).

- **Time to first dollar:** could be day 1 — Stars flow works immediately, effectively no KYC.
- **Payout reality:** Stars → cash has a ~21-day hold. For the league, **Stars received = verified revenue** (screenshot + ledger row); cash lands after week 4.
- **ToS landmines:** Telegram bans spam-inviting; growth has to come from the bot being genuinely shareable, directories, and its own channel.
- **Toolset:** aiogram/grammY scaffold, Stars payments, the daily-report chat is SEPARATE from the product bot.
- **First probe:** smallest playable version of one idea, priced at 1-5 Stars, listed in bot directories.
- **Kill criterion:** day-10 pivot rule as written — $0 stranger-Stars by day 10.

## 4) Prediction markets (Polymarket — the fully closed system)

Give the agent a decision framework + your domain edge; it selects markets and sizes tiny positions.
Spend and earnings live in ONE wallet — the cleanest ledger of all four avenues.

- **Time to first dollar:** first resolved market — could be days, entirely luck-adjacent early.
- **KYC:** wallet setup + funding is the owner's step; **check your jurisdiction's access rules first** (US access has historically been restricted; residency matters).
- **ToS landmines:** jurisdiction + the charter's gambling clause — this avenue is only league-legal where it's legal for the owner. Taxes on crypto proceeds are the owner's.
- **Toolset:** wallet with EXACTLY the budget, market data feeds, a written sizing rule.
- **First probe:** 3 positions × ≤$10 in markets inside your domain edge, each with a written thesis in `decisions.md` (that's what makes it research, not gambling).
- **Kill criteria (non-negotiable, in the charter):** max $10/market, max 3 open positions, stop-loss at −30% of budget. The trading-experiment lesson cuts both ways: 4 of 6 agents lost everything, the "winner" barely played — your edge must be the FRAMEWORK, not volume.

---

**The meta-option** (floated at kickoff): let the agent test a new sub-idea daily inside your avenue.
Honest warning from the session: it burns tokens fast and mostly produces breadth, not depth. If you
run it, cap it: one new sub-idea per day max, each with a kill criterion, kill yesterday's loser first.
